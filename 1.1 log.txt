Beyond/Binary 1.1 release dev log


Bugs- 
* Release has some issues with working, due to tailoff not being implemented in currentSample calculation 
* Slider hitbox occasionally overlaps with other sliders
* Triangle and square waveshapes are missing


Features-
* Full adsr slider support
* Internal midi support
* External midi support (no gui present to switch but the code is there)
* Waveshape swap support, current supported waveshapes are sine and saw


Whats next?-
* Implement other waveshapes 
* Clean up gui
* Add frequency slider and level sliders
